# ZZZ Signal Search Planner

A free, unofficial planning tool for **Zenless Zone Zero**. Enter your current resources and Signal Search progress, choose the Agents, Mindscapes, and W-Engines you want, and see your chances of obtaining them with your current balance or by their deadlines.

[Open the planner](https://grimdarkmagic.github.io/zzzpullcalc/)

## How to use

1. Enter the Monochromes, Polychromes, and Encrypted Master Tapes you want to include.
2. Set your current S-Rank pity and guarantee status for the relevant Channels.
3. Choose your Agent, Mindscape, and W-Engine targets.
4. Arrange your targets in the order you plan to search for them.
5. Review the calculated chances for each target and the possible overall outcomes.

The planner updates automatically as you make changes. Your settings and pull history are saved in your current browser; export your history occasionally if you want a backup or plan to move to another device.

Available in English, Russian, and Japanese, with light and dark themes.
