Predicted vs observed S-Ranks

How this comparison works

Comparison uses the planner's estimated S-Rank pity curve for Exclusive, Rescreening, W-Engine, and Reverberation. Stable and Bangboo are excluded. It measures S-Rank timing and count, not featured-result wins. The soft-pity curve is an estimate; luck labels use 5% and 20% one-sided chance thresholds. Overall chances treat each channel's search count as fixed; stopping after a win can bias the comparison.
